Short Paper: Architectural Incorrigibility for AI Agents through Smart Contracts

Compile in this directory with either:

    latexmk -pdf -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error main.tex

or:

    tectonic main.tex

Without latexmk, the standard sequence is:

    pdflatex main.tex
    bibtex main
    pdflatex main.tex
    pdflatex main.tex

The result is main.pdf: eight pages of main text and two pages of references.
The source uses the unmodified Springer LNCS class and bibliography style.
Standard TeX packages include amsmath, amssymb, microtype, graphicx, xurl,
hyperref, and T1 font support. A first Tectonic build may download packages.

Files:
    main.tex                 manuscript
    references.bib           bibliography
    llncs.cls                official Springer LNCS class, version 2.25
    splncs04.bst              official Springer bibliography style
    SPRINGER-README.txt      publisher template information
    BUILD.txt                these instructions
